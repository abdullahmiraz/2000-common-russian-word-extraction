\markboth{Introduction}{Introduction}

Welcome to \textbf{2000 Most Common Russian Words in Context}. Learning a new language is one of the most rewarding mental journeys, opening the door to a rich culture, history, and literature.

Unlike traditional dictionaries sorted alphabetically, this book is organized strictly by \textbf{frequency of use} in everyday Russian speech and contemporary media.

\section*{Why Frequency Matters}

Linguistic studies show the immense power of high-frequency vocabulary:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=0.4em]
  \item \textbf{Top 1,000 words} account for approximately \textbf{87.8\%} of spoken Russian and \textbf{76\%--80\%} of written text.
  \item \textbf{Top 2,000 words} elevate your comprehension to \textbf{92.7\%} of spoken Russian and \textbf{84\%--86\%} of written Russian.
  \item \textbf{Top 3,000 words} cover nearly \textbf{94\%} of all everyday discourse.
\end{itemize}

By mastering these 2,000 words along with their authentic context sentences, you acquire the practical vocabulary necessary to communicate effectively in Russian.

\section*{How to Use This Book}

Each entry provides six key components:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=0.3em]
  \item \textbf{Frequency Ranking}: Order from \#1 (most frequent) to \#2000.
  \item \textbf{Russian Headword}: Standard dictionary form (infinitive for verbs, nominative singular for nouns/adjectives).
  \item \textbf{Phonetic Transcription}: Easy-to-read pronunciation guide.
  \item \textbf{English Meaning}: Core definitions and grammatical notes.
  \item \textbf{Russian Example Sentence}: Natural sentence showing the word in context.
  \item \textbf{English Translation}: Direct translation of the example sentence.
\end{enumerate}
